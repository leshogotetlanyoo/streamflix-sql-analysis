# Findings

## Revenue

I found that Premium brings in the most money. Each plan has 4 users (Q5), but Premium users pay R199 a month, so the 4 of them bring in R796 of the total R1,242 (Q3), about 64%. Premium also has 0% churn (Q16), so none of that revenue is being lost.

## Churn

I found that Standard is losing the most subscribers, with a 75% churn rate (Q16), followed by Basic at 25%. Premium has 0%. All three users who never watched anything (Q14) have cancelled, which suggests that people who don't use the service are the ones who leave. The data can't tell me why they never watched.

## Engagement

I found that Premium users drive most of the viewing: they account for 14 of the 25 views (Q12), and the top three users by minutes watched are all Premium (Q13). Township Tales is the most-watched show with 5 plays (Q11), and Drama has the most total watch time of any genre (Q10).

## Data quality

The country column has the same country written two ways, 'South Africa' and 'south africa' (Q17). Counted as they are, South Africa would be split into two groups and look smaller than it is. After cleaning, it has 5 users. I would ask the data team to standardise country values when users sign up.

## Recommendation

I recommend that StreamFlix protect its Premium users and try to get Standard users watching more. Premium brings in about 64% of revenue with 0% churn and the most viewing, while Standard has 75% churn. Every user who never watched anything has cancelled (Q14), so encouraging first-time viewing may reduce cancellations.

## Limitation

With only 12 users and 25 views, these patterns are small-sample and could be chance. The data also shows who cancelled but not why.
